# Changelog

All notable changes to DozDefteri are documented here. The format follows
[Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/) and the project uses [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/).

## [1.0.0] - Unreleased

### Added
- Unlimited medications with daily, specific weekdays, every-N-days and as-needed schedules.
- Exact-time dose reminders with Taken / Snooze / Skip actions, rescheduling after reboot, time and time-zone changes.
- Stock tracking with low-stock notifications and days-left estimate.
- Today screen, medication list with archive, monthly history calendar with adherence percentage.
- CSV export (UTF-8 with BOM), PDF report (simple; detailed with Pro), JSON backup and restore.
- Profiles (one free, unlimited with Pro), theme mode and accent colors (Pro).
- Home-screen widget with the next dose (Pro).
- One-time Pro purchase (`pro_lifetime`) with purchase restore.
- Onboarding with notification and exact-alarm permission flows, battery optimization guide per manufacturer.
- English and Turkish translations.
